Two people rated High with one successor between them — the report will say so plainly. That's the point.

What good looks like

Three to six people. Start with the owner; add anyone whose absence would change the plan — including hires you're planning.

Salaries feed Overheads. A $0 owner salary flatters the profit and every bank knows it.

Shareholding should add to 100%. If it doesn't, the ownership table in the report will look wrong to an investor.

Where this goes

People → ownership table and management team. Salaries → Overheads, shown in full or summarised depending on the report. Roles & Capability → management bios (development areas stay internal). Risk & Succession → key-person risk section in funding, SBA and sale reports.
